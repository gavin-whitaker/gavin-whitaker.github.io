AGGIE · est. 2016

Ruth, Ben & Aggie

Ben started baking bread in his USU dorm kitchen sophomore year and never really stopped. Ruth taught sophomore English for nine years and still edits our menu boards for comma splices. Our starter, Aggie, turns ten this year. She's fed every morning at 4 a.m. with flour milled from Cache Valley wheat. We bake small batches, and when it's gone, it's gone. Sorry. (We're not really sorry. Come earlier.)
