I've had curly hair my whole life and spent most of high school at Logan High flat-ironing it into submission. When I opened the studio in 2019 I wanted a place where nobody hands you a brush and a lecture. It's two chairs, a record player, and a lot of good conditioner. Ana joined in 2021 and she's the best curl cutter in the valley. I'll fight you on that.

— Josie. (The name? The willows along the Logan River near First Dam, where I grew up, plus my own hair.)
